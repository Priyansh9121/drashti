# Setup day: the checklist

This is the list for the day Drashti is set up on the mandir's two computers: for the admin doing the setup, with the operators there for the parts marked **(operators)**. Tick each line as it is done, and fill in the "Write down" table at the end. The steps say what to do; `docs/admin-guide.md` says how, and `docs/parallel-run.md` has the first evenings beside the old presentation program.

The old presentation program stays installed and untouched all day. Drashti only reads its files.

---

## Before the day

- [ ] **The audit**, on both computers, if it has not been run yet: `tools/audit/README.md`. Run each script with `--collect` (Mac) or `-Collect` (Windows) onto a USB drive, and keep that drive with you: its copies are raw and can hold licence details and personal data.
- [ ] **The setup checklist** (`tools/audit/SETUP-CHECKLIST.md`), printed and filled in by the operators: the screen map, the features each sabha uses, the keys they rely on, the sound, streaming, Gujarati and Hindi text, and how they switch back.
- [ ] **The OS versions** from the audit reports: the Mac needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, the PC 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. If either is older, stop and decide what to do first; do not update a show computer's OS on the day.
- [ ] **Which computer is Main**, and whether the other is a node or a Main of its own (admin guide, section 1).
- [ ] **The network**: a cable between the computers if possible, and Main's address reserved in the router by whoever looks after the mandir's network (admin guide, section 8).
- [ ] **The installers**: the same Drashti version for both computers, and the version before it, on a USB drive.
- [ ] **A private or unlisted YouTube stream** ready in YouTube Studio for the stream test (never the mandir's public one).
- [ ] **Bring**: two USB drives (one stays plugged in for backups), the key card printed from this version (end of `docs/parallel-run.md`; throw away any older card, as **F5** to **F8** and the logo's key have changed), a notebook, the operators' presentation clicker and any MIDI controller.

## 1. Install (both computers)

- [ ] Install Drashti from its download link (`docs/parallel-run.md`, section 2: the Apple-silicon or the Intel Mac's, or the Windows PC's).
- [ ] **On the Mac, the first time**: open the `.dmg`. Its window shows the two steps, with an arrow from Drashti to Applications: drag **Drashti** to **Applications**, then open it from Applications. If the Mac says Drashti can't be opened, press **Done**, then in **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, press **Open Anyway** beside the message about Drashti, and confirm with the password or Touch ID. **Open Anyway** only shows there after that first try to open Drashti. Once on each Mac.
- [ ] **For whoever looks after Drashti**, instead of **Open Anyway**: once Drashti is in Applications, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Drashti.app` in **Terminal**, and Drashti opens without the question.
- [ ] **On the Windows PC, the first time**: open the installer. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- [ ] **On an Intel Mac**, if the mandir has one (the old program's Mac may be; **About This Mac** shows **Processor**: Intel): install the Intel Mac's file, check Drashti starts, and do the whole rehearsal (section 8) on it too. It has never yet run on a real Intel Mac.
- [ ] On the first start, choose **Main** on the first computer, and **Node** on the second if it will be one.
- [ ] Drashti shows its own logo, not a blue atom: in the Dock and Cmd+Tab on a Mac (on macOS 26 too, if a Mac runs it), in the taskbar, Alt+Tab and the Start menu on Windows. If a Mac still shows an older picture, restart it.
- [ ] Write down the version on each, from the right of the status bar along the bottom of the window (a node's is in Main's screens dashboard, once it is paired). They must be the same.
- [ ] **Check for Updates against the real release** (until now only tried against a test server): **Help**, **Check for Updates…**, then **Check now**, with the internet on (on Windows press **Alt** for the menu). It names this version, 1.0.0-alpha.12, and says **It is the newest.**
- [ ] **On a computer that had an earlier Drashti** (from the parallel-run evenings, or a laptop it was tried on): after this version's first start, the two placeholder presentations, **Language test slides** and **Sample kirtan (placeholder)**, are gone from the library. One that was changed, or used in a playlist, a template or an arti schedule, stays, and the log says why (**Help**, **Save Diagnostics…**). Then make a throwaway presentation (**New…**, a line of placeholder words), open it in the middle, press **⋯** (Presentation actions) beside **Edit slides**, then **Delete…**: Drashti asks first, the middle then says **Nothing chosen yet**, and **Undo** brings the presentation back.

## 2. Bring in the library (Main)

- [ ] Press **Library** and drag the old program's folders (its `.pro6` and `.pro` files) onto its presentation list (`docs/parallel-run.md`, section 3), and any other folders the audit named.
- [ ] Read the report and **write down its numbers**: presentations, playlists, media, problems.
- [ ] **Media that could not be found** (the report's notice "… media files could not be found"): **Find missing media…**, and pick each folder the files are in (or **Find…** beside one file).
- [ ] **Media Drashti cannot play**: its videos and sounds convert by themselves, one at a time, a moment after the import. Each says **Converting…**, and cannot go live, until its copy is made, and the report says what was converted. HEVC video (as phones record) converts the same way on a computer that cannot play it, such as a Windows PC without Microsoft's HEVC extension; where it can, it plays as it is. Once, quit Drashti while one is still converting, and start it again: it says **Converting…** again, and those left start by themselves about 10 seconds after the window opens (never while the stream is on air or recording). Let them finish. For what is left (the notice "… media files Drashti cannot play", pictures such as HEIC among them; and in **Media**, any HEVC video still offering **Convert**): **Convert all**, and let it finish in the background.
- [ ] **Legacy fonts** the report's notes name (under **Imported with notes**): install each font on **Main and on the node** (admin guide, section 11). Quit and start Drashti again, and look at a few of those slides.
- [ ] **Words from Word files**: two of the mandir's own `.docx` files of a kirtan's words (they stay on these computers), one typed in Unicode and one in a legacy font. Drag each onto the library: each becomes a presentation, a slide for each block of lines, with the right letters; the report's notes name the legacy font, and its slides look right once that font is installed.
- [ ] **PowerPoint, Keynote and PDF decks**: drag them in. Each page becomes a picture slide. The first time, macOS asks whether Drashti may control Keynote or Microsoft PowerPoint: **Allow**. If the report says there is no Keynote or PowerPoint here, or that one could not open a deck, save the deck as PDF and drag in the PDF.
- [ ] **Keynote and PowerPoint on this computer** (never tried on these machines before the setup). With a placeholder deck (a few slides of made-up words, one hidden slide, one with an animation, speaker notes on some), not one of the mandir's: drag it in, and check the report says which app saved it as PDF, counts the hidden slide and the animation, and that the slides' notes are right. Then check that the app closed again (or, if it was already open with something of yours, that it was left as it was). **On the Windows PC with PowerPoint** this is the first time it has ever run: also check PowerPoint showed no window, and drag in a deck while PowerPoint is open with an unsaved presentation: the presentation must still be there, unsaved. Write down how long the deck took (the log says, **Help**, **Save Diagnostics…**).
- [ ] **On the Windows PC with PowerPoint, a deck with a macro**: a placeholder deck holding a macro that shows a message, saved as a PowerPoint 97–2003 `.ppt`. Drag it in: its slides come in as pictures, the message never shows, and PowerPoint's own macro setting (in its **Trust Center**) is as it was.
- [ ] **Import from a Link with a real Dropbox link** (never tried against Dropbox itself: the tests use a stand-in). With a Dropbox link to one of the mandir's own short videos, and one to a mandir folder holding a placeholder `.pptx`, a short MP4, a placeholder PDF and a few pictures, some of them in a folder inside it: **File**, **Import from a Link…**, **Dropbox**, paste, **Download**, into a test folder (admin guide, section 14). Write down: whether each link was looked at (its name and size shown), saved and imported; that the folder came as a playlist of its own, its inside folder under a header, and its PDF and pictures came in with **Add the PDFs and pictures**; and anything the window said. If Dropbox refused, write its exact words. Then the same on the other computer, if it is a Main too.
- [ ] **Import from a Link with one of the mandir's own YouTube videos** (never tried against YouTube itself: the tests use a fake yt-dlp). An admin, with a short video from the mandir's own channel: **File**, **Import from a Link…**, **YouTube**, paste its Share link, choose **MP4** at **1080p**, tick the box, **Download**, into a test folder; then the same video as **MP3** (admin guide, section 14). Once only on each computer that is a Main. Write down: whether the title and length showed, the qualities offered, how long each took, whether each was saved and imported and plays, and anything the window said (its exact words if it refused).
- [ ] **A 4K video on the Windows PC** (nobody has measured one there): in the same window, set **Highest video quality** to **2160p (4K)**, and download the same video at **2160p** (YouTube sends it as VP9 or AV1). Put it up as a background for a minute on the screens the PC drives: write down whether it plays smoothly on each. Then set **Highest video quality** back to **1080p**.
- [ ] **The drop folder**, if the mandir will use one (admin guide, section 18): **File**, **Drop Folder…**, **Choose…** the folder (choosing it turns it on). Put a picture, a short video and a sound in it: a few seconds later each is in **Media** by itself, under **From the drop folder**, and a notice says how many came in. If it is a synced Dropbox or OneDrive folder, try it there: Drashti warns that a folder on this computer alone is safer, and still takes each file only once it has all arrived.
- [ ] **A smart playlist on a USB stick**, if the mandir will use one (admin guide, section 15): **+ New**, **Smart Playlist from a Folder…**, and a folder on the stick holding a few placeholder files. Put one more file in: it joins at the end by itself. Take one out: its item says **Not in the folder**, and still plays. Pull the stick out: the line under the playlist's name says the folder is not there, and nothing is lost; put it back, and it carries on.
- [ ] **(operators)** Open five kirtans they know well and compare with the old program: the words, the line breaks, the Gujarati and Hindi letters, the backgrounds.
- [ ] **Back up**: **File**, **Back Up Library…**, with the media, to the USB drive.
- [ ] **Scheduled backups**: **File**, **Scheduled Backups…**, onto the drive that stays plugged in (admin guide, section 5). Press **Back up now** once and check it finishes.

## 3. Screens and sound (Main, then the node)

- [ ] **Set Up Screens…** (in **View**): **Show each display's number on it**, then for each display what it shows (one click) and its languages, as the screen map in the checklist says, and **Done**. (With only the hall's display connected, **Show on** it under the top bar does the hall in one click.)
- [ ] **Keep these screens?** In **Set Up Screens…**, **The controls are on this display** marks the operator's display: check it is the right one. After **Done**, Drashti asks for 15 seconds: **Keep** (Enter) or **Go back** (Esc). Try **Go back** once: the screens are as they were, and **Set Up Screens…** shows the choices again to put right. Then press nothing once: after 15 seconds the screens go back by themselves, and a notice says so. In **Screens**, changing what a group **Shows** (or what a key and fill screen **Sends**) asks too. Once, while it asks, stop Drashti as a power cut would, with a forced quit (on a Mac **Option+Cmd+Esc**, **Drashti**, **Force Quit**; on Windows, in PowerShell, `Stop-Process -Name Drashti -Force`), and start it again: the screens are as they were before that change, and a notice says so. Last, **Done** and **Keep**.
- [ ] **Sound** (**Set Up Screens…**, then **More setup…**): the output to the mixer, and **Play a test tone**. **(operators)** Check the mixer hears it on the right channel.
- [ ] Canvas sizes and scaling for any screen that is not 1920 × 1080 (an LED wall).
- [ ] **(operators)** **Looks**: the everyday Look first in the list, then any others they need ("Gujarati only", "Lower thirds").
- [ ] **(operators)** **Stage layout**: with the performers, the boxes they want on the stage screen.
- [ ] **(operators)** **The hall's picture on a stage screen**, if the mandir wants it under the stage message, as the old program showed it: in their stage layout (**Edit stage layouts…** under the stage group in **Screens**), choose **The audience picture, live** in the list of boxes beside **Add**, then **Add**. It goes under the boxes already there, so the stage message draws over it. Place and size it, and **Save**. On the stage screen it matches the hall's screens: the slide, the background, black-out and the logo. With **Audience** off it keeps the picture and says **Audience off** in its bottom left corner, where a stage message along the top does not cover it (a box of the audience screens' state says "Audience screens off"). On a node with no hall screen of its own that is on, it shows Main's hall as the live Look has it. A tablet's stage display (section 5) shows it too, each picture or video in it as a small still (a video input or a window is black there, named in a small line in the box's bottom left corner). With no audience group in **Screens** at all, the box is black and says **No audience screens**, and so does a box of the audience screens' state.
- [ ] **(operators)** **The audience picture box at its smallest**: in the layout editor, drag a corner of **The audience picture, live** in as far as it goes: it stops at 320 × 180, as its **Width** and **Height** say. **Save**, and look at the stage screen from where the performers stand. Turn **Audience** off: **Audience off** can be read in the box; turn it on again. Then, for a moment, change what the hall's group **Shows** in **Screens** to **The stage view (performers)**: while Drashti asks whether to keep it, with no other group showing **The audience picture**, the box says **No audience screens**, as does a box of the audience screens' state if the layout has one (**The audience screens (black-out, logo)** in the list of boxes). Press **Go back**. Last, size the box as the performers want it, and **Save**.
- [ ] **Masks** for any screen that is not a rectangle, or a projector that spills: draw each over the hall screen's live picture in the mask editor, so it fits what is really there.
- [ ] **Key and fill**, if the mandir's video switcher lays words over the camera: set the switcher's key to **pre-multiplied**, and check a lower third keys cleanly over a camera, with no white edge round its words, as it comes in, changes and goes out.
- [ ] **Audience and Stage** (Main): the two switches in the top bar beside **Screens**, or **Cmd+1** and **Cmd+2** (**Ctrl+1** and **Ctrl+2** on Windows), which **View** shows beside its **Audience** and **Stage**. With a slide up, turn **Audience** off (Drashti asks first: **Turn off**): the projector shows the computer's desktop, and the status bar says **Audience off**; turning its screen off and on again in **Screens** meanwhile asks nothing. Turn it on: the hall's picture comes back full-screen on the same display, asking nothing. Do it once from **View**, **Audience**, too. Then **Stage** off (**Turn off** again) and on: each of the three stage screens shows the desktop, then the stage view again on its own display. If key and fill is used, **Audience** turns its two feeds off and on too. Last, with an output put over the controls (Drashti asks first: **Cover the controls**, then **Keep**), **Cmd+1** asks on that output too, large in its middle and readable from the hall: **Esc** cancels; **Cmd+1** again and **Enter** turns Audience off, and the controls are back. Turn it on again, and **Cmd+Shift+U** (**Ctrl+Shift+U**) takes that output off the controls. The show, the stream and the node carry on throughout.
- [ ] **The video mixer as a video input** (Main): the ATEM's program, through its USB output into Main. **Library**, **Media**, **Add a Video Input…**. On the Mac, unless Drashti may use the camera already, it says why first: **Continue**, then **Allow** when macOS asks (if it was refused: **Open System Settings**, turn **Drashti** on under **Camera**, then **Look again**). On Windows, if Drashti says Windows' camera privacy setting is off: **Open Settings**, turn on **Camera access** and **Let desktop apps access your camera**, then **Look again**. The mixer shows by its name, with its small live picture: choose it, keep the name **Input 1**, and **Add to Media**. It is listed under **Video inputs** at the top of **Media**. Write down the name the mixer showed under. If the second computer is a Main of its own, or the library may go to another computer, name that computer's video input **Input 1** too: playlists and slides name the input, and where no input has that name they say so (**Not on this computer**). If it is renamed later (its **Change** in **Media**), this computer's playlists and slides take the new name with it: give the other computer's input the same new name, or there they say **Not on this computer**.
- [ ] **What a new name does** (Main): press **Change** at the end of **Input 1**'s row in **Media** (the pencil). Before a new name is typed, a muted line under **Name** and **Fit** says that this computer's playlist items and slides that use it take the new name, and that the spare computer's input needs the same new name. Type a new name (**Input 2**): the dialog keeps its size and place, with **Cancel** and **Save** in sight on the operator's display. Then **Cancel**: it is still **Input 1**.
- [ ] **The node** (if there is one): **Pair a node** on Main, give the node's displays their groups, and tick **Get everything ready** in the screens dashboard. In the screens dashboard, the node says **Online** and its media all ready.

## 4. The show's own things (Main)

- [ ] **The logo**: in **Props**, the stamp button beside the mandir's logo prop.
- [ ] **(operators)** Props, message templates and timers they use, from the checklist's "Features used".
- [ ] **(operators)** Any MIDI pads (**More tools**, **MIDI controller…**, **Learn**).
- [ ] **The idle rotation**: pictures and quotes from authorised sources, and which screens show it.
- [ ] **Music** lists for before the sabha, and **markers** on any video or sound that needs a start or end point.
- [ ] **(operators)** **Markers brought over from the old program**: open three or four videos or sounds that had start or end points (or markers) there, and check in their **Markers** that Drashti's are at the same times. They follow community notes on the file formats, never yet checked against the mandir's own files: write down any that are off.
- [ ] **Shastra texts** and **calendars**, if the mandir has prepared files from an authorised source.
- [ ] **Sabha templates**: replace the two examples with the mandir's real running orders.
- [ ] **(operators)** **The keys** follow what the operators already press: **F1** to **F4** as before (Clear all, the slide, the background, props), **F5** the sound and **F6** messages, **F7** the ticker and **F8** masks (each pair changed places from earlier versions of Drashti), **F12** the logo (**L** too), **Cmd+1** the audience screens off and on, and **Cmd+2** the stage screens (**Ctrl+1** and **Ctrl+2** on Windows). **Help**, **Keyboard Shortcuts…** lists them all. The bar along the bottom has their buttons in the same order, each showing its key: **Clear all** F1, **Slide** F2 to **Masks** F8, then **Logo** F12 and **Black-out** B. Look at it on the operator's display at the size the window is used, with **Black-out** and the **Logo** on too: every word and key can be read, and nothing scrolls sideways. Try each, and their presentation clicker (Next, Back, Black-out), and write down any key they still want different.
- [ ] **(operators)** **F12 on the mandir's own keyboards**, on each computer: **F12** puts the logo up and takes it off, and does nothing else. On a laptop it may need **Fn**, as **F1** to **F8** do: on a Mac, unless **Use F1, F2, etc. keys as standard function keys** is on (**System Settings**, **Keyboard**); a Windows laptop may need **Fn Lock** on. Check no other program on these computers takes **F12**, **Cmd+1** or **Cmd+2** (**Ctrl+1** or **Ctrl+2**) for itself: with Drashti in front, only Drashti answers each, and once (one press of **Cmd+1**, one question; hold **Cmd+1** down for a second: one change only, or one question; in a field, such as the library's search box, **Cmd+1** and **Cmd+2** do nothing). Write down what each keyboard needs.

## 5. Phones and the network (Main)

- [ ] **Phones**, **Let paired phones and tablets connect**. Answer the firewall: **Allow** on the Mac; on Windows **Private networks** only, and check the mandir's network is set as private.
- [ ] Write down the address the Phones panel shows, and check it is the reserved one.
- [ ] Pair two or three phones as remotes, and a tablet as a stage display. Phones need Safari 16.4 or later (iOS 16.4) or Chrome on Android.
- [ ] **A presenter's iPhone and iPad** (`docs/presenter-guide.md`): pair, Add to Home Screen, open it from the Home Screen, search the library for words on a slide, open the presentation, tap a slide (the screens follow), read the notes, and hold the iPad sideways (the notes and Next beside the slides). Try a Windows laptop's browser too if a presenter uses one.
- [ ] **The phones through a restart**: with a phone's remote and the tablet's stage display open, quit Drashti on Main and start it again. While it is closed, the phone says **Not connected** and the tablet shows its last picture, saying it is not connected; once Drashti is back, both connect again by themselves. Without reloading either page, put a slide up on Main: both show it at once, with what comes next, and the phone's **Next** moves the show on, the tablet following.
- [ ] Make the announcements poster, print it, and send one test announcement.

## 6. Roles and PINs (Main, and the node's Main settings)

- [ ] **File**, **Roles and PINs…**: an admin PIN and an operator PIN.
- [ ] Give the operator PIN to the operators; keep the admin PIN with the admins. **Never write a PIN in the shared notebook.**
- [ ] Check: quit and start Drashti; it opens in Pro Mode, the header's padlock saying **Operator** (the padlock alone below 1680 px wide: see the next line), with no PIN asked. Then **Simple Mode** in the header, and **Switch to Pro Mode…** (top right) with the operator PIN switches back to Pro Mode.
- [ ] **The top bar** on Main, with Phones on and the PINs set, at the size the operators use the window: every button can be seen and pressed, nothing is cut off or over another, and nothing scrolls sideways. Below 1680 px wide, **Themes**, **Stream**, **Phones**, **Announcements**, **Simple Mode** and **Operator** (or **Admin · Lock**) show their icon alone, each saying what it is when the mouse rests on it; **Screens**, **Audience** and **Stage** keep their words. Below 1280 px, the network's badge shows how many devices alone beside its phone (its words when the mouse rests on it) and the **?** beside Stream, Phones and Announcements are gone. On the computer that streams, check it on air and recording too: **ON AIR** and **REC** fit, and nothing is over a button.

## 7. The stream (the computer that streams)

- [ ] Stream settings: the key from YouTube Studio (pasted once; Drashti never shows it again), the camera, the mixer's line in (or the video mixer's USB output, which can be the stream's **Sound input** too), **Good** or **Weak internet** from a speed test. Then, in the Stream panel's recording part, **Choose a folder…** for the recordings.
- [ ] Write down the **Encoder** line the Stream panel shows (a graphics encoder such as VideoToolbox, NVENC, Quick Sync or AMF is best).
- [ ] **On the Mac that streams**: during the test stream, write down the frames a second the Stream panel shows (it should hold at the profile's 30). CI's Mac, a virtual machine, managed only about 9: a real Mac has never been measured.
- [ ] **Go live** on the unlisted stream for ten minutes while changing slides and playing a video, and for a minute with **Input 1** up and the stream's **Layout** on **Slides**. In YouTube Studio: the picture (Input 1 as the hall sees it, a slide's words over it), the sound (from the video mixer's USB output, if it is the **Sound input**), the lips in time with the words (else raise **Sound delay**).
- [ ] End it, open the recording in VLC, and do it all a second time on another day before a real sabha.

## 8. The checks

On each computer:

- [ ] **The performance check**, twice (`docs/parallel-run.md`, section 11). Copy the second line into the table below. It should say `"passed":true`.
- [ ] **The performance check's video cases** (same section): `video-1080p30` and `dissolves-video` on every computer that shows video (Main and the node), and `masks-video` where a screen has a mask. In each line, "every screen showed 9 in 10 of the background video's frames" must say `"ok":true`, and the slide-change lines too, "the import overlapped at least 2 slide changes" among them (on a quick computer it passes with "nothing to judge": the import was over too soon to need it, and the slide-change lines judge the five changes from its start instead, saying how many came after it). A computer that keeps fewer than 9 in 10 frames needs a graphics chip that decodes video (admin guide, sections 1 and 13).
- [ ] **On the Windows PC: ahead of other programs, or level with them** (admin guide, section 13). Run `video-1080p30` and `dissolves-video` again with `$env:DRASHTI_PRIORITY = 'normal'`. Keep Drashti ahead (the default) unless the normal runs kept clearly more of the video's frames (with dissolves) and their slide changes still passed; then an admin unticks **File**, **Run Ahead of Other Programs** on that PC (press **Alt** for the menu). Write down which, and why.
- [ ] **The watchdog self-test**, on Main and on the node (admin guide, section 12): every line PASS. On the node, with a slide of words up on Main and the node's display showing it.
- [ ] On the PC: **`docs/windows-checks.md`**, every line Pass, Fail (with what you saw) or N/A.
- [ ] **A video background on every screen** for a minute, with a dissolve to another: smooth on every screen, the node's too, and in step. The first video after installing or updating can stutter for a second while the computer prepares its graphics: put one up once before each first sabha.
- [ ] **The node in step**: a dissolve and a video side by side on Main's screen and the node's.
- [ ] **(operators)** **A whole sabha rehearsed**: in Pro Mode by an operator, and in Simple Mode by a volunteer who has not used Drashti before. Write down anything they found hard.
- [ ] **By keyboard alone** in Simple Mode: Tab to each big button, and Enter; and to **Switch to Pro Mode…** at the top right.
- [ ] **A screen reader**, if anyone at the mandir uses one (this needs a person who uses it every day): VoiceOver on the Mac (**Cmd+F5**) or Narrator or NVDA on Windows. Check the regions are announced (header, playlists, slides, live, show controls, status, and the library while its sheet is open), the buttons say what they do, the dialogs say their names, and Simple Mode's buttons can be found and pressed. On a phone, VoiceOver or TalkBack on the remote: Next and Back, and the tabs.
- [ ] **On the Windows PC: Windows' own text size at 200%** (Windows 11: **Settings**, **Accessibility**, **Text size**; Windows 10: **Settings**, **Ease of Access**, **Display**, **Make text bigger**; 200%, then **Apply**): the operator window's buttons, lists and dialogs still fit and can be read, and nothing is cut off. Set it back afterwards if the operators prefer.
- [ ] **The fallback drill**: the named fallback operator quits Drashti and opens the old program. Time it: it should take under a minute.
- [ ] **(operators)** **Zoom sees the stream's picture**, with a placeholder slide, not a real meeting: in the Stream panel, **Window for Zoom…**. **Drashti for Zoom** shows exactly the Stream panel's picture with nothing else on it, keeps its shape when dragged bigger from a corner, and opens there again next time; closing it changes nothing on the screens or the stream.
- [ ] **(operators)** **A window on the hall's screens**, with a placeholder window, not a real meeting (a TextEdit or Notepad window with a few placeholder words): on a Mac, turn on Screen Recording first (admin guide, section 4). **Library**, **Media**, **Show a Window…**: it lists that window by name with its picture (never a whole screen or Drashti's own), and **Show on the screens** puts it on the hall's screens, fitted with black around it. **B** covers it, and **F3** takes it off. Minimise it while it is up: the screens keep its last picture and the yellow notice says so; **Clear the window** takes it off.
- [ ] **(operators)** **Input 1 on the screens**, with the video mixer on a camera: one click on **Input 1** under **Video inputs** in **Media** puts it up, dissolving in over a second, on LIVE, the hall's screens and the stream's **Slides** layout, with a slide's words over it. With no slide up, stage screens say **Video input on the screens**; the node's screens show the rest of the show without it, as the screens dashboard says. **B** and **F12** cover it, and **F3** takes it off. Drashti shows only its picture, never its sound: the hall's sound comes through the mixer, as now.
- [ ] **(operators)** **Input 1 in a playlist, and behind a slide**: right-click **Input 1** in **Media** and add it to the open playlist (or drag it there): its card says **Video input**.
