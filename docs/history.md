# What is built, and when

> Drashti is built independently for use at a BAPS mandir. It is not an official BAPS product.

Until 26 Nov 2026 this was the README's "Status" section. It moved here whole, word for word, so that the README starts with what a newcomer needs. It says what is built, part by part, with the session or release that built it.

- Names in quotes, such as "Soak test" or "Access by Gmail", are sections of the [README](../README.md), and "below" means there. Those of its "Running a show", such as "Simple Mode", "The stage screen" or "The time bar", are in [running-a-show.md](running-a-show.md) since 26 Nov 2026.
- What each release changed, in plain words: [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).
- The newest release: https://github.com/Priyansh9121/drashti/releases/latest.

Phase 0 (foundations) is in place:

- the read-only **audit kit** for the two show computers (`tools/audit/`);
- the **show engine** in the main process: versioned, serializable state sent as snapshots and patches through a transport interface;
- the **SQLite library** with the full core model, migrations and two placeholder presentations (only for the tests since Session 50: a new library starts without them);
- **screens**: displays as the OS reports them, screen groups, a canvas size and scaling per screen, and one output window per assigned display, restored on restart; since alpha.20 each screen's picture zoomed, moved and corner-pinned (keystone), and two to four projectors joined into one wide picture with gamma-correct edge-blend ramps (**Adjust the picture…**, **One wide picture…**);
- one **shared renderer** with bundled Noto fonts, used by the operator preview, the thumbnails and every output;
- a minimal **operator UI** with a single keymap, and an **output watchdog**.

Phase 1 has started:

- text boxes hold **styled runs** (font, size, colour, weight and language per run);
- the **import pipeline** runs in a background worker process: plain-text and Word (`.docx`) lyrics, .pro6 and .pro presentations, templates and themes, playlists and bundles, and media files, with re-import rules, a media folder that stores each file once, and a report kept for every import;
- **importing from the operator window**: drag files or folders onto the open **Library** sheet or anywhere on the window, or use **Import…**; progress, then a migration report with a fix for each item; removing presentations with Delete, and Undo;
- **arrangements** set the order a presentation plays in (a repeated chorus shows and plays each time), chosen per presentation or per playlist item;
- **playlists** in the operator window: imported ones with their folders, headers, media and placeholders, and the operator's own, built by dragging from the library or with Add to playlist, and put in order with Up and Down;
- **playlist playback**: a kept shuffle and Repeat all, totals and Ends at, Find that never puts a row up, and local-file M3U import and UTF-8 export;
- **a playlist's running order** (alpha.22; `src/main/running-order.ts`): **⋯ › Copy running order** puts plain lines on the clipboard for the sabha team (the main process writes them), headers as sections, every other row numbered with its length, and the time in all; **Print running order…** and **Save running order as PDF…** put it on one A4 list from the manual's hidden, locked printing window (`src/main/manual-print.ts`: one page at a time across the manual and every running order), refused while anything is up, on air or recording; Pro Mode's;
- **search** as you type, by title and slide text in English, Gujarati, Hindi and transliteration, ignoring Latin accents, with v and w alike and a doubled vowel as its single one ("dvitiyah" finds "dwitiyah", "Shri" finds "Shree"; Session 13), x as ksh, f as ph, and ru as ri after a consonant ("Axar" finds "Akshar", "Fal" finds "Phal", "Krushna" finds "Krishna", but "guru" never finds "giri"; Session 14) (about 4 to 5 ms a query at 5,000 presentations); text in legacy fonts is left out, and the results say so;
- **prepared translations (alpha.21)**: **Edit slides… › Translate…** compares manually written Gujarati, Hindi and English words with their source, requires a named reader's exact check, and saves independent output choices with reserved space below the original. Drafts, changed words and translations that cannot fit stay hidden. **Prepare translations** (alpha.22, `src/main/translation/`) drafts the empty ones through `drashti-translate` (CTranslate2 4.9.1 + SentencePiece 0.2.1, built by `scripts/fetch-translate.mjs`; `LICENSES/translation/`) with an imported model pack's IndicTrans2 model (format `indictrans2-ct2-int8`), Pro Mode and off air only, drafts unchecked; without them **No engine installed** is explicit; the deterministic translator is used only by tests. Simple Mode reads the saved status and plays checked words without changing them;
- **editing words as plain text**: any presentation's words in the lyrics format, saved back with each slide's look, backgrounds and cues kept, the live slide updated and Undo; "New…" makes a presentation from pasted words; since Session 55 only New… opens it, the open presentation's **Edit words** having gone with the slides area (words are changed in **Edit slides**);
- **Screens that set themselves** (Session 55, `src/main/outputs/auto-screens.ts`, `display-ids.ts`): a display plugged in is used at once, with no question and no click: the first that is not the one with the controls (nor a computer's own built-in display) shows the audience picture, the next the stage, any more the audience picture; one line under the header says so, with **Change…** (Screens, at that display: its job in one click, **Audience**, **Stage** or **Not used**). Each display is remembered by itself, by its EDID maker, model and serial number (read off the main thread: system_profiler on a Mac, PowerShell on Windows), so it goes straight back to its job when plugged in again, into another port, or after a restart, and two projectors of one model are told apart; a job changed by hand is remembered instead, Not used too.
