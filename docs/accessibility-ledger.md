# Accessibility ledger: WCAG 2.2, Levels A and AA

Every WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA success criterion, once, for Drashti's own interface: the operator window (Pro Mode and Simple Mode) with its dialogs, sheets and menus, the node's window, and the pages for phones and tablets (pairing, the remote, announcements, the stage display). The audience screens and the stream draw the mandir's own content (its slides, pictures, videos and sound), so they come in only where a criterion reaches them (2.3.1, and 2.2.2 for the previews).

Each criterion has one status:

- **Pass**: it holds, and the test named shows it.
- **Fail**: it does not hold; the gap is said.
- **N/A**: nothing in Drashti's interface it applies to; why is said.
- **Needs a person**: no test here can settle it; what a person should check is said.

Written in Sessions 31–36 (A11Y-3), 17 October 2026. Change a row when the interface or its tests change.

**The scans.** `tests/e2e/a11y.ts` runs axe-core with the `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa` and `wcag22aa` rules: 170 checks in 59 spec files (some in loops, so more scans run), every panel, dialog and page. A serious or critical finding fails its test, and so does a moderate finding not accepted, with why, in `tests/e2e/a11y-moderate.json` (none is accepted today). "axe" below means those scans. The pictures of the screens (marked `data-a11y-picture`: the previews and the Program) are left out of them, as they draw the show's own slides.
