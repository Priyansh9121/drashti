# What changed in Drashti

Each version, newest first. A version's section is what Help › Check for Updates… and its release page show, so it is written for the people who run Drashti, in plain words: only "- " lists, no other formatting, and at most 4,000 characters. Changes not released yet wait under Unreleased, at the top; a release gives them their version's heading.

## 1.0.0-alpha.18 (14 Nov 2026)

Update after a sabha, and update Main and nodes together.

- A Trim cut that carries on after a restart says Preparing on its row from the start, and Stop lets it go. If one can't carry on, the notice as Drashti starts says why. An earlier cut nothing uses goes to the Trash with its still frame, and a notice names it.
- Where a computer draws without a graphics chip, the stream's picture no longer stays black for up to 8 seconds as it starts. Drashti's log says how long each hall screen took to get ready.
- Import notices say each thing once: another app's reason no longer repeats the app's name, a Keynote deck whose PDF pages are in another order says so for its videos too, and an automatic import names its new points once.
- PowerPoint slides look more as they do in PowerPoint: theme colour shades, styled shapes' and backgrounds' gradients, Medium Style 2 tables, outline widths and table corners, and a list's indents through a change of size. A picture fill Drashti can't copy is no longer drawn as a flat colour.
- Edit slides keeps a deck's other pages editable when one page can't be read or the deck has more than 128 MB of pictures, and a page left as a picture says plainly why.
- Lower Thirds has 18 more templates, 68 in all.
- A phone's Save of a slide waits while the hall screens move, so a Next just after it stays smooth on a two-core laptop; when it saves a picture from the slide instead, it says so.
- Names of playlists, audio playlists, wallpapers, screens and screen groups leave out hidden characters copied with them, as Rename… does, and a name with nothing visible is refused. Rename's Undo says the name as it was kept.
- Lower Thirds has twelve more moving backgrounds, calm ones first (Light rising, Lamps glowing, Petals falling and more), then livelier ones. One box in the studio finds an animation or a template by any words, such as "slide up slow" or "calm".
- A PowerPoint text box keeps PowerPoint's own shrink of its words and lines. Edit slides shows a box's line spacing as drawn, and warns of close Gujarati or Hindi lines.
- On a two-core Windows computer, an import while streaming no longer holds the library for seconds, which could hold up a slide change for up to 2 seconds.
- The stream, the decks Drashti plays itself, the start and the Edit slides fixes since alpha.17 (this list is written in full before the release).

## 1.0.0-alpha.17 (13 Nov 2026)

Update after a sabha, and update Main and nodes together. Bring in files that need converting before a sabha.

- No import asks a question or opens a report, in either mode. What you bring in goes into the library and the open playlist, or the library alone from the Library sheet. A changed file is kept beside the earlier one.
- Anything that did not come in has a small notice saying why, with Try again or Find… where they help, as do a calendar's or book's left-out parts, a deck another app had to make and a file whose points an import changed. A smart playlist's own import never moves what is playing.
- An import names the old Gujarati or Hindi fonts it meets: install them on Main and each node.
- From a Link… takes a pasted YouTube or Dropbox link into the open playlist at once, at up to 1080p, a Dropbox folder with its PDFs and pictures. The Highest video quality setting is gone.
- Converting waits while anything is on the screens or playing, or the stream is on air or recording; a file that needs it cannot go live until then.
- Trim on a playlist's video, in either mode, makes a new video of just that part, exact to the frame, for that row only, which says Preparing and plays the original until it is ready. The original never changes: Trim again widens the cut, and Reset to original puts it back. A cut Drashti closed before making carries on after the next start.
- Edit slides reaches every object: click again for the one underneath, or use the Objects list. Double-click a text box to type, even under a shape. Right-click for Cut, Copy, Paste, Group and more. Add a picture from the computer.
- Edit slides keeps a PowerPoint slide's bullets, numbering, spacing, tables and their styles, fills, outlines, vertical words and hidden objects. Charts, SmartArt and very large pages stay pictures. On a Mac a Keynote deck is edited through Keynote's PowerPoint copy (its PDF when the two differ). A big PDF opens at once on the page you chose.
- A font this computer lacks is drawn in the nearest one it has: look over long slides.
- Drashti plays a PowerPoint deck's own transitions and animations itself, on Mac and Windows, even without PowerPoint, most exactly as PowerPoint does: Push, Cover, Uncover, Fly, Float, Split, Grow & Turn, Grow/Shrink and Basic Zoom among them. The Wipe transition, Shape and any effect it cannot match play PowerPoint's video where one can be made, else the closest effect, a notice naming the slide.
- A PowerPoint slide keeps its clicks after an edit, even one imported before alpha.17, videos too where they match.
- A deck's clicks start on the press and play their full length. Jumping straight to a PowerPoint slide plays its first animation whole on every screen; a screen that was slower to start plays a little faster for a few seconds, so the screens and the slide's sound come back together.
- The opening waits, black, for Drashti's window to start underneath, then plays its two seconds smoothly.
- The screens no longer stay black at a start or restart: up to 8 seconds without a graphics chip, or for good if getting a screen ready went wrong. A slide with a big imported table no longer stops them for a moment. After a crash, the last show up comes back, even if Windows refused a save for a moment.
- The Wallpaper and Black-out turn each other off, every way in. A slide or picture under them comes back when the cover comes off.
- A phone with the computer in Simple Mode does what Simple Mode's window does: any playlist, Pause, Play, the time bar and When it ends.
- Audio on the phone, in both modes, plays and changes the audio playlists and their tracks, with Undo, loop and shuffle.
- Rename… leaves out hidden characters copied with a name; Gujarati and Hindi joiners stay.
- Lower Thirds' Templates is a gallery of moving previews, with search, filters, Best 20, Favourites and Recently used.
- 40 new name-and-role lower thirds that move in several parts, a line, then the name, then the role.

- Double-click Simple Mode to switch to it, and double-click Pro Mode to switch back; one click only says so. With Simple-only access Drashti stays in Simple Mode.
- Notices are small and see-through in the bottom-right corner: what is under them still shows.
- Simple Mode has Pro Mode's playlists on the left: several, in folders, with smart playlists, each saying in a line what it is. Templates, sharing, Duplicate and Repeat Slides stay in Pro Mode.
- A folder dragged onto Drashti becomes a smart playlist at once, with nothing asked. Deleting the open or last playlist closes it; Undo brings it back open.
- Simple Mode has Record under the time bar and Pro Mode's Audio under it. Slide, Background and Audio sit beside Clear all, and the line of keys is gone: every button shows its key.
- In Simple Mode a row's right-click menu has Trim (never a copy), Trim black and silence, Even loudness, When it ends, and Edit slides… for a presentation, PDF or deck.
- Simple Mode's From a Link… is Pro Mode's whole: quality, Save in, which playlist, a Dropbox folder as a playlist of its own, Show in Finder and the import report. The permission tick is gone.
- A PowerPoint or Keynote deck shows its slides as soon as their pictures are ready: 60 slides in about 6 s with PowerPoint on a Mac, where it took over a minute. Slides with animations say Preparing until their clicks are ready, and Next waits for them. On a Mac the animations are in sooner too (60 slides: PowerPoint 69 s to 46 s, Keynote 138 s to 102 s). A PDF's pages are drawn several at once while nothing is up and the stream is off.
- On a Mac, Drashti no longer looks in PowerPoint's folder at every start, so macOS no longer asks about another app's data.
- The opening is two seconds: one photo, no words. An opening pack may hold the mandir's own sound (sound.mp3, .m4a or .wav), played gently on this computer only. File > Opening… shows it and takes it out.
- Live captions hear Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit or English. Choose one, or "as heard", which leaves out other languages. Sanskrit shows in Gujarati letters.
- The five AI helpers from alpha.15 are removed. Find passages by typing in the library's Books tab. Saved playlists and loaded Books still work.
- Record sound saves the chosen sound input as a WAV file (16 kHz mono, speech quality); with captions on, a timed draft goes beside it. Record video saves the stream's picture and sound, even off air. Choose the folder in Pro Mode.
- Windows copies include the libraries the captions engine needs.

It keeps the play order, never jumps to another verse and never advances by itself: the operator presses Next. Both speech helpers need Live captions switched on for listening; neither turns it on for you.
- A backup no longer stops for one missing or damaged original document: it keeps the rest and names what it left out.
- What you delete stays deleted, even from a playlist that follows a folder or the drop folder.
- With no opening pack, the opening shows a built-in photo of Swamishri (photograph: BAPS), zooming gently, with no words.
- Drashti's window no longer waits at start for the answer to whether this computer plays HEVC video.
- Back and Next share the width equally, panel headings use their full width, and each mode keeps its own Undo.
- The admin and operator guides and setup-day checklist cover all five helpers and their separate choices.

## 1.0.0-alpha.13 (29 Oct 2026)

From this version, Drashti opens only for people the owner has let in, and needs the internet to check. Each computer is let in once, under one Gmail (a mandir computer under the Gmail the owner chooses, an operator's own computer under their own), with a one-time token from the owner's email, and roles and PINs are gone. Update after a sabha, never on the day of one, and the node computers the same evening.

- Access by Gmail: a computer not let in shows Request Access and I Have a Token. The owner approves by email, and the token, typed in once, lets that computer in until the owner revokes it or its length ends. Drashti checks every 15 seconds: revoked, it stops at once (screens black, the stream and recording ended, nodes and phones stopped). With no internet it keeps working, the stream and recording too, for up to an hour, then stops. Keep a wired connection, and a phone's hotspot as a spare.
- On the owner's computer, View › Access List… shows every Gmail with its status, dates, computers and counts, to sort, search and save as CSV, and to add, approve, reject, revoke, give access again or change a length.
- How long access lasts: Unlimited (chosen at first), 1 day, 7 to 365 days or a number of days. The Sheet and the Access List show the end, a bar in Drashti says it from 7 days before, then Drashti stops until more time is given. Give mandir computers Unlimited.
- Roles and PINs are gone: everyone let in can do everything. Leave Simple Mode with its button and the word pro.
- Stage layouts: New from a Template… makes a timer layout in one click (Timer only, Countdown to a time, Countdown, Count up, or Timer and clock over the audience picture). Every box moves, resizes, overlaps and goes to the front or back, with its own fill, see-through, bold, outline or shadow, size, colour and alignment. A timer box has its format, warning and over colours and a gentle flash at 0:00; a clock, 12 or 24 hours and seconds. The Stage screen panel and the phone have the layout's timers with Start, Pause and Reset.
- The time bar: under LIVE, in Music and on the phone, a bar for each thing playing, with its time and time left, its start and end points and markers. Click or drag to move there on every screen, with Play and Pause. Simple Mode shows it and never moves it. A video on a slide can show a play bar on the screens, off until ticked.
- PowerPoint and Keynote decks come in with their animations on a Mac with either app (on Windows, once File › Decks With Their Animations on This Computer is ticked): Next and Back step through each click on every screen, and the deck's videos and sounds play as the deck says, with the time bar, its music on across slides as in its slideshow. Drashti's own transitions can go over or instead of the deck's. A deck that came in as pictures before asks before it is replaced. Without either app, a deck's videos and sounds still come in.
- One click on a playlist's video, picture or sound puts it up at once (Cmd-click, or Ctrl-click on Windows, only chooses it; Alt-click shows it without putting it up). A download from a link goes into the playlist chosen last, or asks you to name one, and opens it.
- Phones and tablets: pictures, slides and pages as sharp as the device allows, two fingers to zoom, and a button to put the live zoom on the screens. The remote has what the operator window has, setting up screens, video inputs, windows, picture in picture, the library (with Undo), masks, MIDI and announcements. A playlist can be shared with phones by right-click, and with Phones and tablets may add files turned on, a phone adds pictures, videos, sounds, PDFs and decks (a PowerPoint deck as .pptx) into Added from Phones.
- A missing sound is named on the slide's picture, in the editor and in Simple Mode's Next. The stage box's lines naming an input or window stay readable. Two quick ticks of a box never undo each other. An import that gives a file new start or end points moves what is up at once.

Streaming and quitting

- A Mac can now go live to YouTube. In 1.0.0-alpha.0 the Mac could not check YouTube's secure connection, so the stream never went on air. Windows was not affected.

- The quit question now says when the stream is on air or recording, and what will stop.
- After a quit on purpose, Drashti starts again off air and not recording. Only after a crash does the stream come back by itself (within 5 minutes, as before).

- A screen that fails while drawing now goes black and comes back by itself, instead of staying blank for the rest of the sabha. If the operator window fails, it offers to reload itself, without touching the screens.
- A screen that freezes is now noticed and restarted.
- A screen Drashti has given up on is tried again after 2 minutes, then every 10 minutes. Meanwhile it shows as Stopped in Screens and the status bar, with Try again.
- Dissolves no longer dim, flash or start from black: when slides change quickly, a background dissolving in carries on to its end; a slide still loading its pictures never flashes up; and a screen that opens part-way through a dissolve shows the background whole.

Starting up, and output nodes

- After an unexpected stop 3 hours or more before Drashti starts again, it now starts as after a normal quit (in Pro Mode, or Simple Mode when PINs are on), not in Simple Mode.

- Some rare errors when quitting during an import, or with a node paired, are fixed.
- In Drashti's log, a hidden stream key now reads [stream key] once, not "[stream key] key]". The key itself was never written.

Updating

- Windows: Download, then turn on "Install it when Drashti quits". It installs as Drashti quits after the sabha.
- Mac: Download, then Show the file. After the sabha, quit Drashti, open the file and drag Drashti into Applications.
- Update the output nodes to the same version the same evening.
